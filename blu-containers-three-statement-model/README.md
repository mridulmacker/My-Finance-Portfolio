# Blu Containers — Three-Statement Financial Model

An integrated Excel model for a hypothetical manufacturing company, linking the income statement, balance sheet and cash flow statement across three historical years (2020–2022) and a five-year forecast (2023–2027).

Completed as part of the CFA Institute Financial Modeling Practical Skills Module, using the module's Blu Containers case study and practice workbook.

**[Download the Excel model](Blu_Containers_Three_Statement_Model.xlsx)** · **[View the PDF](Blu_Containers_Three_Statement_Model.pdf)**

## What the model covers

- Revenue forecasts driven by selling prices, sales volumes, freight costs and factory capacity.
- Production costs and SG&A forecasts incorporating inflation assumptions.
- Working capital schedules based on receivable, inventory and payable days.
- Capital expenditure, depreciation, debt, interest, tax and equity schedules.
- Integration of all three financial statements, with balance sheet checks.
- Base, best and worst cases for pricing, inflation and sales volume growth.

## Commercial insights

- **Capacity limits growth:** base-case production reaches the factory's 420,000-unit annual capacity in 2027, limiting further volume growth without expansion.
- **Pricing affects margins:** base-case net revenue falls from $281.2m in 2025 to $268.2m in 2027 despite rising volumes. EBITDA margin falls from 30.7% to 20.3% as selling prices decline and costs rise.
- **The scenarios show earnings sensitivity:** 2027 net income is approximately $19m in the base case, $30m in the best case and $5m in the worst case.

These are outputs of the case study assumptions, rather than forecasts for a real company.

## How to use it

1. Download and open the `.xlsx` file in Microsoft Excel.
2. Review the **Assumptions** and **Scenarios** sheets.
3. Change **Scenarios!D6** to select a case:

   | Value | Scenario |
   | --- | --- |
   | 1 | Base |
   | 2 | Best |
   | 3 | Worst |

4. Review the linked financial statements and supporting schedules in **Model**, then compare case outputs in **Summary**.
5. Check the balance sheet reconciliation in **Model**, row **188**, after changing assumptions. A zero indicates that assets equal liabilities plus equity.

The PDF provides a static view of the model and scenario summaries. Use Excel to change inputs and recalculate outputs.
